Got questions?
We’ve got answers.

Here’s everything you need to know to get the most out of Oxbow.

What is Oxbow and how can it help my team?

Oxbow is a collaborative workflow platform that centralizes tasks, files, and communication in one intuitive interface, so your team stays aligned and productive.

Is there a free trial or plan available?

Yes, we offer a 7-day free trial with full access to all features, plus a free plan for small teams to get started at no cost.

How secure is my data on Oxbow?

We use industry-standard encryption for data at rest and in transit, with regular security audits to ensure your information is always protected.

Can I integrate Oxbow with other tools I already use?

Absolutely! Oxbow supports integrations with Slack, Google Drive, Zapier, and more—so you can keep your existing workflows connected.

How do I upgrade to a paid plan?

Upgrading is simple: just visit the Billing section in your account settings, choose a plan that fits your needs, and you’ll be all set.
